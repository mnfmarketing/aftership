Questions firms ask

How we work, in plain terms

The short version of everything on this page — for the associate who just wants the facts.

Does working with Injury Bridge cost my firm anything?
No. Treatment is provided on a lien basis — your client is treated first, and we're paid from the settlement. There's no upfront cost to your firm or your client.
What happens if the case doesn't settle?
Every case carries risk, and lien terms address exactly this. Talk to your Injury Bridge representative about how a specific case is handled.
How quickly can a client be seen?
Most clients are evaluated within 48 hours of referral, with same-day availability in many areas.
What states do you cover?
We're licensed to coordinate care in all 50 states, so your client is covered regardless of where the incident occurred.
What types of care are available?
From initial evaluation to imaging, specialist referrals, prescriptions, and mental health support — we coordinate the full continuum of care your client needs.
How do I send a referral?
Call our live intake line at (855) 785-7585 or submit a referral through the provider portal — our team responds immediately.
